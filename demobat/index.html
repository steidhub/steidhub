      About BAT

      BAT play heavy metal the old way: loud, fast and sharp-edged. Born in Richmond, Virginia, the band has put out a demo, an EP and three full-length albums, and in 2026 paid tribute to Venom with Live Like An Angel (Die Like A Devil). Their records come out on Nuclear Blast.
